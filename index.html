About

I am a 5th year Ph.D. student in mathematics at Johns Hopkins University working with David Savitt . My research interests are in algebraic number theory and arithmetic geometry, particularly p-adic Hodge theory, Iwasawa theory, and the p-adic Langlands program. I am very interested in Eigenvarieties and the Halo behaviour of Eigenvarieties.
